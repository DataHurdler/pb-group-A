## How each slide is written

- **On slide:** the text that appears on screen. At most 25 words including
  the title, one idea per slide.
- **Notes:** timing, talking points, common student mistakes (*Common
  mistake* or *Watch for*), and facilitation tips (*Tip*).
- **Layout:** on the title slide, section dividers, and YOUR TURN slides,
  which UNO-branded layout to use and the logo's alt text. Layouts and
  accessibility rules are in [../brand/README.md](../brand/README.md); brand
  values are NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- **Exercise slides** start with `YOUR TURN · ⏱ N MIN` and a working format.
  The full exercise (task, time, format, template or starter prompt, and
  suggested answers marked *instructor only*) follows the slide.

## Every module follows the same pattern

1. **Warm-up** question or poll
2. **Concept**, 15 minutes or less of presenting
3. **Live demo** by the instructor
4. **Hands-on exercises**, at least 40% of the module
5. **Share-out and questions**, the last 10 minutes, ending on a recap slide

## Ground rules built into every exercise

- **Made-up data only.** All names, companies, and figures are fictional
  (Brightline Supplies, Priya, Marcus, Jordan Lee). Learners use invented
  details for their own tasks too.
- **Free tools.** Everything works on the free versions of ChatGPT, Claude,
  and Copilot. Techniques are tool-neutral.
- **Demos never promise a specific output.** Each demo's notes say what to do
  whichever way the run goes.

## Before teaching

- Rehearse the four demos (slides 16, 31, 45, 58) on the room's Wi-Fi and your
  chosen tool.
- Print: Exercise 1 cards (one set per pair), the Exercise 7 texts, the
  bake-off brief and Exercise 9 scenarios (one per table), and the handout (one
  per learner).
- Add your feedback-form link or QR code to slide 87.
- Check the resource searches on slide 88 still lead to the official guides.
