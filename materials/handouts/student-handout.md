# Prompt Engineering for Work — Your One-Page Guide

**Prompt:** what you type into an AI tool. **LLM:** the technology behind ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot. **It predicts, it doesn't look up:** it writes what sounds likely and only knows what you tell it.

## The five parts of a good prompt

| Part | Ask yourself | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Role** | Who should it act as? | "You are an experienced customer service manager." |
| **Context** | What's the situation? Who's it for? | "A long-time client's order arrived two weeks late. They're upset." |
| **Task** | What exactly should it do? | "Write a reply apologizing and offering 15% off their next order." |
| **Format** | What should it look like? | "Under 150 words, short paragraphs, end with a next step." |
| **Constraints** | What are the rules? | "Warm but professional. Don't blame the courier. No jargon." |

**Three techniques:** **Show examples** ("Format like these: …") · **Ask for steps** ("Work through this step by step, then answer") · **Iterate** (follow up: *Make it shorter · More formal · Give me three options · What did you assume? · What's missing?*)

**Four-question check, before you use any output:** ☐ **Accurate?** facts, numbers, names ☐ **Complete?** everything asked, nothing important missing ☐ **Fair?** no biased or exclusionary wording ☐ **Fit for purpose?** right tone, length, audience

**Before you paste:** 🟢 public → fine · 🟡 internal → check your policy · 🔴 confidential or personal → don't. Swap details for placeholders: *"Maria Chen, acct 48213" → "[Customer], [account]"*. Today: made-up details only.

## Exercise templates

**Ex 2 · Test the limits:** for each prompt, note *What worked · What was wrong · How could you tell?*

**Ex 3 · Fix-it:** rewrite A *"Write a job posting."* B *"Summarize this."* C *"Make this email better: hey, the report is late again, need it asap."* using at least three of the five parts.

**Ex 4 and 11 · Your task (reusable template):**
> You are a [role]. Context: [situation, audience, why it matters]. Task: [action verb + what to produce]. Format: [length, structure]. Rules: [tone, must include, must avoid]. Example of the style I want: [optional]. Details for this time: [what changes each time].

**Ex 5 · Show, don't tell:** "Turn these notes into action items, one per line, exactly like: *Priya — Send budget draft — Friday*. No owner? Write UNASSIGNED."

**Ex 6 · Iteration log:** Round 1 / 2 / 3: *What I changed → Better, worse, or same? Why?*

**Ex 8 · Bake-off scoring:** score each group 1–5 on Accurate · Complete · Fair · Fit for purpose.

**Ex 11 · Capstone checklist:** ☐ five parts ☐ one technique ☐ iterated twice ☐ four-question check ☐ privacy check ☐ [brackets] for reuse
